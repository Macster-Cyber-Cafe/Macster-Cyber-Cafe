2025 KJSEA Results: Simple Guide for Parents & Students

The 2025 Kenya Junior School Education Assessment (KJSEA) results have been officially released by the Kenya National Examinations Council (KNEC). This assessment marks an important transition for Grade 9 learners moving into Senior Secondary School under the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC).

Official KJSEA Results Portal

All KJSEA results are accessed through the official KNEC portal. Parents and students are advised to use only the official website to avoid misinformation.

https://kjsea.knec.ac.ke

What You Need Before Checking Results

  • Assessment Number (9 or 10 characters)
  • At least one candidate name (first name or surname)
  • A device with internet access
  • Stable internet connection

How to Check 2025 KJSEA Results

  1. Visit the KJSEA Portal: Open your browser and go to the official KJSEA website.
  2. Enter Assessment Number: Carefully type the learner’s assessment number and confirm it is correct.
  3. Enter Candidate’s Name: Provide at least one name of the candidate for verification.
  4. Read the Privacy Notice: Review the data protection and access notice before proceeding.
  5. Click “Search Results”: Submit the details to retrieve the learner’s results.
  6. View and Print Results: Confirm the details, then download or print the result slip for future use.

Need Help with Results or Printing?

If you experience difficulties accessing results, downloading files, or printing documents, you can visit MACSTER CYBER for professional assistance.
