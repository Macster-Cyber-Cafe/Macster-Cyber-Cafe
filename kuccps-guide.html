KUCCPS & KMTC March 2026 Intake

The Kenya Universities and Colleges Central Placement Service (KUCCPS) has officially opened applications for the Kenya Medical Training College (KMTC) March 2026 intake.

This application is open to KCSE candidates from the years 2000 to 2024 who wish to pursue medical and health-related courses.

Application Deadline: 27th January 2026

Note: Candidates who sat for KCSE in 2025 will apply in subsequent intakes and are not included in this round.

Who Can Apply

  • KCSE candidates from 2000 to 2024
  • Applicants who meet the minimum course requirements
  • Those interested in KMTC medical courses

How to Apply for KMTC March 2026 Intake

  1. Visit the KUCCPS Student Portal
  2. Log in using your KCSE index number and examination year
  3. Select available KMTC courses
  4. Submit your application before the deadline

Official KUCCPS Portal: https://students.kuccps.ac.ke
